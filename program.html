About Open Work Inc

Open Work Inc is a developer platform created to connect talented programmers, provide modular architecture blocks, and foster open-source collaboration worldwide.

Founded by Cody Bunnell, our mission is to make developer resources accessible, clear, and high-performance.

Platform Architecture Details
  • Boilerplates: Modern starter templates utilizing Bootstrap 5 and modular vanilla frameworks.
  • APIs & Modules: Fully documented endpoints for swift integration.
  • Community Support: Direct channels to engineering leads and contributors.
