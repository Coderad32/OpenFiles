Platform Security Infrastructure

At Open Work, safeguarding your projects, source code, and developer credentials is our highest priority. We implement robust, multi-layered security frameworks across our entire infrastructure:

  • Container Isolation: Every development workspace runs inside a heavily sandboxed, isolated micro-VM to prevent cross-container interference.
  • End-to-End Encryption: All data in transit is encrypted using advanced TLS protocols, and data at rest is secured via AES-256 bit encryption standards.
  • Access Controls: Fine-grained token management and multi-factor authentication (MFA) ensure only authorized collaborators access your environments.
  • Vulnerability Scanning: Continuous automated audits check dependencies and repositories for common vulnerabilities and exposures (CVEs).
Privacy Policy & Data Handling

Open Work is built by developers, for developers. We believe your data belongs to you. This privacy policy outlines our data processing commitments:

  • Minimal Data Collection: We only track metrics necessary for provisioning workspaces, matching you with relevant job opportunities, and ensuring optimal platform performance.
  • No Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal user data, source code, or telemetry logs to data brokers or third-party advertisers.
  • Code Confidentiality: Your code repositories remain strictly confidential. Our automated systems analyze code syntax purely for workspace configuration and never use private repos to train public models without explicit consent.
  • User Rights: You retain the right to request a complete export or deletion of your account history and personal data at any time via your settings dashboard.
Cookie Policy & Preferences

To deliver a smooth, reliable development and hiring platform, Open Work uses cookies and similar tracking technologies. Here is how we categorize them:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for fundamental site features such as session authentication, secure workspace logins, and user preferences.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Help us analyze how developers interact with our workspace deployment tools so we can optimize load times and interface layouts.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember custom configurations, such as your dark/light theme choices and terminal shortcut maps.
  • Managing Cookies: You can block or delete cookies via your browser settings; however, disabling essential cookies may impact your ability to log in or deploy workspaces.

Community Code of Conduct

Open Work is dedicated to cultivating a welcoming, collaborative, and diverse community of developers, engineers, and creators. We expect all participants to uphold these core standards:

  • Inclusivity & Respect: Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or personal attacks based on gender, race, religion, sexual orientation, or technical skill level will not be tolerated.
